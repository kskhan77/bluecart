Welcome to Blue Cart Check

You will look at photos of everyday items and help us answer one question: does this item go in the City of Flint's blue recycling cart?

Your answers become the labels of a new dataset. Later, a computer model will learn from them. Thank you for helping.

What you will do

1Look at the photo

Judge the main item, exactly as it looks. You can zoom in.

2Step 1: the kind of item

Is it a kind Flint recycles (blue bin), a kind it never takes (black bin), or can't you tell?

3Step 2: its state

Only for blue bin items: is it ready, does it need a quick fix, or is it ruined?

4Next photo

Press →. Your answers are saved automatically.

Step 1: what KIND of item is it?

Blue cart item 1Paper, cardboard, cans, foil trays, plastic bottles, jugs and tubs, glass bottles and jars.
Never the blue cart 2Bags, cups, styrofoam, food, batteries, electronics, cords, and anything else not on the blue list.
Can't tell 3Blurry, cut off, or no single main item.

Step 2: what STATE is it in? (only after the blue bin)

Ready 4Empty, clean, loose, flat.
Needs prep first 5Rinse it, empty it, flatten it, or take it out of the bag.
Ruined 6Grease or food soaked into paper or cardboard.
Can't see enough 7The photo does not show its state.

An example

A greasy pizza box. Step 1: it is cardboard, so choose the blue bin. Step 2: the grease has soaked in, so choose Ruined. The tool then shows your answer: Not accepted. A clean, flat pizza box would be blue bin, then Ready: Accepted.

Three things to remember

  • Use Flint's rules, not your hometown's. Flint takes foil trays and empty aerosol cans. It does not take cups.
  • Judge only what you can see in the photo. Don't guess what the item looked like before.
  • If you are about 70% sure, pick the answer. Use "can't tell" only when the photo itself blocks the decision.

Good to know

  • Plan about one hour. Stop after an hour even if photos are left.
  • The rules are always on screen. The Guidelines button at the top opens the full rules with examples.
  • A line above Step 1 always tells you what to do next and what your answer means.
  • If you close the browser, log in with the same name and you continue where you stopped.
